Third-party software
====================

This port contains no vendored third-party source code. Dependencies are
fetched by Cargo and are all under permissive licenses (MIT and/or Apache-2.0,
zlib, BSD). Run `cargo about` or `cargo license` for a full list when
redistributing binaries, and ship their notices alongside.

Main dependencies: wgpu, naga, eframe/egui, egui-wgpu, transform-gizmo-egui,
gltf, image, glam, bytemuck, clap, rfd, flume, anyhow, log, env_logger, pollster.
